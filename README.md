### Key Business Insights Highlighted

1. **Revenue Seasonality & Growth:**
   - E-commerce sales peaked in **November 2017 (Black Friday)**, generating the highest monthly sales before stabilizing at sustained run-rates across 2018.

2. **Core Revenue Drivers:**
   - Revenue is concentrated within the top product categories: `health_beauty`, `watches_gifts`, and `bed_bath_table`.

3. **Logistics & Customer Experience (Critical Impact):**
   - **On-time deliveries** maintain an average review score of **~4.29 / 5.0**.
   - **Late deliveries** cause ratings to plummet to **~2.57 / 5.0**, demonstrating that shipping reliability is the single greatest driver of customer churn and negative sentiment.

4. **Regional Disparities:**
   - Southeastern states (SP, PR, SC) demonstrate low late delivery rates (~2.8%–5%).
   - In contrast, northern and northeastern states (MA, AL, AP) experience late delivery rates exceeding **20%–23%**, indicating a need for regional fulfillment hub optimization.

5. **Payment Behavior:**
   - Credit cards account for the vast majority of transaction volume and enable installment-based purchases (averaging ~3.5 installments), followed by Boletos and Vouchers.
